SecondLook AI is a purchase-decision intelligence application. It helps users evaluate products using product information, images, user preferences and AI-generated research.

1. Who we are

SecondLook AI is developed and published by Not Another AI. For privacy questions, users may contact us at noanotherai@gmail.com.

2. Information SecondLook AI processes

Product photos

When you scan or select a product photo, the image may be transmitted securely to Google Gemini through Google's Firebase AI services for product identification and analysis requested by the user.

SecondLook AI does not upload product images to Google Cloud Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage as part of its application data storage. Product images used by the app are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.

Product information and text

Text you enter, and information extracted from a product image, may be sent to Google Gemini and Google Search services used by the application to identify products, research pricing and product information, and generate the requested purchase analysis.

AI analysis and saved reports

SecondLook AI can generate product evaluations, including fair-value estimates, purchase verdicts, alignment factors, friction or risk factors, alternative products and grounded research sources.

Saved reports are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage by SecondLook AI.

Account and authentication identifiers

SecondLook AI currently uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication for app usage and entitlement operations. This creates a Firebase user identifier (UID). A locally generated installation identifier is also used to help enforce usage and entitlement protections against reinstall abuse.

The app does not require users to create a traditional username/password account or sign in with a Google Account to perform normal product analysis, use a free evaluation or use a paid subscription.

Usage and entitlement information

To enforce free and paid usage limits, the app stores limited entitlement and usage metadata. This may include the Firebase UID, installation ID, entitlement tier, evaluation identifiers used for consumption tracking, and usage timestamps or counters.

Google Play purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. SecondLook AI does not receive or store payment card numbers, bank account details or billing addresses.

The app stores only the entitlement state necessary to determine access to paid features. Google Play processes the underlying payment transaction.

Device and network information

Standard Android and Google services may process technical information required to operate secure network requests and Play Integrity verification, such as device characteristics, Android version and network information.

SecondLook AI does not intentionally store IP addresses, device serial numbers, IMEI numbers or advertising IDs in its application database.

Information we do not intentionally collect

  • No precise or approximate location data.
  • No advertising ID for advertising or behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No payment card or bank-account information.
  • No contact-list or phonebook data.

3. How information is used

  • Identify and evaluate products at the user's request.
  • Provide AI-generated purchase-decision intelligence.
  • Research product and pricing information using Google Search services.
  • Maintain usage limits and entitlement status.
  • Protect free evaluations and paid access from abuse.
  • Process and verify Google Play subscriptions and purchases.
  • Maintain account and service security.

4. Third-party services and data sharing

SecondLook AI uses Google services to provide core functionality. Depending on the feature used, relevant information may be transmitted securely to:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — anonymous authentication and user identifiers.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — limited usage and entitlement metadata.
  • Google Gemini through Firebase AI services — product images and product text needed for AI analysis.
  • Google Search services — product and pricing research requested by the app.
  • Google Play Billing — purchase and subscription processing.
  • Google Play Integrity — app/device integrity verification for security.

SecondLook AI does not sell personal information and does not share user information with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Local storage

The following information may remain on the user's device: product images, saved product decisions and reports, user decision preferences, category profiles, display-currency and theme preferences, onboarding state, and locally cached entitlement or usage state.

6. Security

Data transmitted between the app and supported cloud services is sent using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

Release builds use Firebase App Check with Play Integrity as an additional application-security measure. Access to Firestore usage records is restricted by Firebase security rules.

7. Data retention

Locally stored product images, saved reports, preferences and cached application data remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app storage, uninstalls the app or otherwise removes them through available app controls.

Limited Firebase authentication, usage and entitlement records are retained only as needed to provide authentication, enforce entitlements, protect against abuse and operate the service, subject to the deletion process below.

If information must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal or regulatory purposes, only the information reasonably necessary for that purpose may be retained for the applicable period.

8. Account and data deletion

SecondLook AI provides an in-app Delete Account & Data option under Settings → Account & Privacy.

When an in-app deletion request succeeds, the app deletes the Firebase Authentication anonymous account, the associated Firestore user record, the associated installation/entitlement record, and locally cached account or usage state managed by the application.

Locally stored product images, saved reports, preferences and other application data can also be removed by clearing the app's data through Android settings or available app controls.

Request deletion without access to the app

If you no longer have access to SecondLook AI, you can request deletion of your SecondLook AI account and associated cloud data without reinstalling the app.

Send an email to noanotherai@gmail.com with the subject SecondLook AI — Data Deletion Request.

Request deletion by email

If available, please include your SecondLook installation identifier or Firebase identifier so that we can locate the applicable records. If you do not have the identifier, state that in your request and provide any other information available to help us identify the applicable SecondLook records.

We will process valid deletion requests within 30 days, subject to any information that must legitimately be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal or regulatory purposes.

Where applicable, deletion requests will cover SecondLook AI cloud records associated with the request, including the Firebase Authentication identifier, associated Firestore user data, and installation or entitlement records.

Deleting a SecondLook AI account does not delete the user's Google Account and does not cancel or delete Google Play subscription records. Google Play manages those records separately.

9. Children's privacy

SecondLook AI is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the app's functionality.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.
