1. Who we are

SecondLook AI is developed and published by Not Another AI. For privacy questions, data inquiries, or account assistance, users may contact us at noanotherai@gmail.com.

2. Information SecondLook AI processes

  • Product photos: When you scan or select a product photo, the image may be transmitted securely to Google Gemini through Google's Firebase AI services for product identification and analysis requested by the user. SecondLook AI does not upload product images to Google Cloud Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage as part of its application data storage. Product images used by the app are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Product information and text: Text you enter, and information extracted from a product image, may be sent to Google Gemini and Google Search services used by the application to identify products, research pricing and product information, and generate the requested purchase analysis.
  • AI analysis and saved reports: SecondLook AI can generate product evaluations, including fair-value estimates, purchase verdicts, alignment factors, friction or risk factors, alternative products and grounded research sources. Saved evaluation reports are stored locally on your device in the app's private database and are not uploaded to Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage by SecondLook AI.
  • Account and authentication identifiers: SecondLook AI uses Firebase Authentication for app usage, account security and entitlement operations.
    • Anonymous Authentication: Creates a temporary Firebase user identifier (UID) for initial app navigation and local feature configuration.
    • Linked Google Account: Claiming your one lifetime free complete evaluation report requires linking a permanent Google Account. This provides a verified Google account identifier used exclusively to enforce free trial anti-abuse protection.
    • Pseudonymous Anti-Abuse Identifier: A trusted backend service generates a pseudonymous HMAC-SHA-256 identifier from your verified Google Account subject ID using a cryptographic secret securely maintained in Google Secret Manager. These records do not store your raw Google Account subject ID, Google password, email address or profile name. They contain limited technical eligibility and security metadata. Although pseudonymous, such records may still be considered personal data under applicable privacy laws.
    • Device Installation Identifier: A locally generated device installation ID is used alongside server-side device records to enforce usage limits and protect against reinstall abuse.
  • Usage and entitlement information: To enforce free and paid usage limits, the app stores limited entitlement and usage metadata in Firebase Cloud Firestore. This includes your Firebase UID, device installation ID, pseudonymous Google identity anti-abuse hash, evaluation reservation identifiers, entitlement tier, and usage timestamps or counters.
  • Google Play purchases: Purchases and report credit packs are processed through Google Play Billing. SecondLook AI does not receive or store payment card numbers, bank account details or billing addresses. The app stores only the purchase token and credit balance state necessary to grant access to paid features. Google Play processes the underlying payment transaction.
  • Device and network information: Standard Android and Google services process technical information required to operate secure network requests and Play Integrity verification, such as device characteristics, Android version and network information. SecondLook AI does not intentionally store IP addresses, device serial numbers, IMEI numbers or advertising IDs in its application database.

Information we do not intentionally collect

  • No precise or approximate location data.
  • No advertising ID for advertising or behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No payment card or bank-account information.
  • No contact-list or phonebook data.

  • Research product and pricing information using Google Search services.
  • Maintain usage limits, credit balances and entitlement status.
  • Protect the one lifetime free complete report offer and paid features from abuse.
  • Process and verify Google Play purchases and credit packs.
  • Maintain account and service security.

4. Third-party services and data sharing

SecondLook AI uses Google services to provide core functionality. Depending on the feature used, relevant information may be transmitted securely to:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — authentication and user account identifiers.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — limited usage, credit balance and entitlement metadata.
  • Google Secret Manager — secure key management for pseudonymous anti-abuse HMAC generation.
  • Google Gemini through Firebase AI services — product images and product text needed for AI analysis.
  • Google Search services — product and pricing research requested by the app.
  • Google Play Billing — purchase and credit pack processing.
  • Google Play Integrity — app/device integrity verification for security.

SecondLook AI does not sell personal information and does not share user information with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Local storage

The following information remains on the user's device in local application storage: product images, saved product decisions and evaluation reports, user decision preferences, category profiles, display-currency and theme preferences, onboarding state, and locally cached entitlement or usage state.

6. Security

Data transmitted between the app and supported cloud services is sent using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Release builds are configured to use Firebase App Check with Play Integrity as an additional application-security measure to verify app and device integrity. Access to Firestore usage records is restricted by Firebase security rules, and pseudonymous Google identity anti-abuse records are accessible exclusively by trusted backend services.

7. Data retention and anti-abuse records

  • Local Storage Retention: Locally stored product images, saved reports, preferences and cached application data remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage through Android Settings ("Clear Data"), or uninstall the app.
  • Personal Cloud Usage Data: Firebase authentication records, usage reservations and credit balance records associated with your active SecondLook account are retained while your account remains active.
  • Pseudonymous Anti-Abuse Records: To enforce the one lifetime free complete report policy across account deletion and app reinstall, minimal pseudonymous anti-abuse records (the device installation ID and the pseudonymous Google Account HMAC hash) are retained while this offer protection remains active. These records do not store your raw Google Account subject ID, Google password, email address or profile name. They contain limited technical eligibility and security metadata. Although pseudonymous, such records may still be considered personal data under applicable privacy laws. These records have no automatic expiration and are subject to an annual internal review of continued necessity.

8. Account and data deletion

SecondLook AI provides an in-app Delete Account & Data option under Settings → Account & Privacy.

When an in-app account deletion request is confirmed:

  1. Deleted Cloud Data: The app permanently deletes your cloud SecondLook account, associated Firebase Authentication user record, Firestore user profile data, active usage reservation records, and cloud purchase ledger entries for that account.
  2. Forfeited Paid Credits: Deleting your SecondLook account permanently forfeits any remaining paid report credits on that account. Paid credits cannot be refunded or transferred after account deletion.
  3. Retained Anti-Abuse Records: To prevent repeated claims of the one free complete report, minimal pseudonymous anti-abuse eligibility records (your device installation ID and pseudonymous Google Account HMAC hash) remain retained in server storage. These records do not store your raw Google Account subject ID, Google password, email address or profile name, but contain limited technical eligibility metadata.
  4. Local Device Data: Locally stored product photos, saved reports and category preferences stored in your device's local app database remain on your device until you clear app storage through Android Settings ("Clear Data") or uninstall the app.
  5. Google Account & Play Subscriptions: Deleting your SecondLook account does NOT delete your actual Google Account and does NOT automatically cancel active Google Play subscriptions. Google Play manages subscriptions separately.

Request account deletion without access to the app

If you no longer have access to the SecondLook AI app, you can request deletion of your SecondLook AI account and personal cloud usage data without reinstalling or opening the app. Send an email to noanotherai@gmail.com with the subject SecondLook AI — Account Deletion Request. You do not need to know or provide your Firebase UID or installation identifier in your initial email. To verify your identity and locate your account, our team may request reasonable additional information. Confirmed deletion requests cover personal SecondLook cloud account data, subject to minimal pseudonymous anti-abuse eligibility records (device installation ID and pseudonymous Google Account HMAC hash) retained for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

12. Google Play purchases and credit packs

Purchases in SecondLook AI are conducted through Google Play Billing.

  • Report Credit Packs: Users may purchase consumable packs of 5 complete evaluation reports for US$3.99 (or local equivalent). Multiple packs may be purchased.
  • Consumable Credit Accounting: Purchased report credits are consumed as reports are generated. Deleting your SecondLook account permanently forfeits any remaining unused credit balance.
  • Payment Security: All payment transactions are processed directly by Google Play. SecondLook AI never receives or stores payment card numbers or bank account details.
