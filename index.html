Privacy Policy

SecondLook AI — Before you buy, take a SecondLook.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

SecondLook AI ("SecondLook", "we", "our", or "the app") is a purchase-decision intelligence application developed and published by Not Another AI.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SecondLook AI processes, why it is processed, where it is stored, and how users can request deletion of account-related cloud data.

Privacy by design: Product photos, saved product decisions, reports, profiles and preferences are primarily stored locally on your Android device. SecondLook does not operate its own advertising or behavioral tracking system.

2. You Do Not Need to Create an Account

Normal use of SecondLook AI does not require you to create or sign in to a Google account.

SecondLook uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a temporary Firebase user identifier for certain cloud usage and entitlement operations. This identifier is not your name or email address.

Google Play handles purchases and subscriptions separately through the Google Play Billing system.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Firebase Anonymous User ID

SecondLook may create an anonymous Firebase Authentication user ID. This identifier is used to associate cloud usage and entitlement information with an application session.

3.2 Installation Identifier

SecondLook generates a locally created installation identifier. This identifier may be stored in Firebase Firestore to help enforce free-use and entitlement limits and protect against repeated reinstalls being used to bypass those limits.

3.3 Product Images

If you photograph a product or select a product image from your device, the image may be processed by Google AI/Gemini through the Firebase AI/Vertex AI service for product recognition and analysis.

Product images are also stored locally in the app's private storage when required for the application's decision and report features.

SecondLook does not upload product images to its own Firestore database or its own cloud storage.

3.4 Product Information and Text

Product names, descriptions, specifications, prices and other text that you enter or that is extracted from a product image may be sent to Google AI/Gemini for analysis.

SecondLook may also use Google Search Grounding to research current product information, prices and related evidence.

3.5 AI Analysis Results

SecondLook generates purchase-decision information such as fair value estimates, verdicts, alignment factors, friction or risk factors and grounded sources.

These decision results and saved reports are stored locally on your device. SecondLook does not upload your complete saved reports to Firestore.

3.6 Profile and Preferences

Information such as budget preferences, priorities, dealbreakers, use contexts, category profiles, display currency and application preferences is stored locally on your device.

3.7 Usage and Entitlement Information

SecondLook stores limited usage and entitlement metadata locally and, where required, in Firebase Firestore. This may include the entitlement tier, usage counters, installation identifier and identifiers used to prevent duplicate evaluation consumption.

3.8 Google Play Purchase Information

SecondLook uses the Google Play Billing system for subscriptions and paid entitlements.

SecondLook does not receive or store your credit card number, bank account details, billing address or other payment-card information. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

4. Information We Do Not Collect for Our Own Purposes

  • Advertising ID
  • Precise location
  • Approximate location
  • Phone contacts
  • Payment card numbers
  • Bank account information
  • Behavioral advertising profiles
  • Crash-reporting data through a separate crash SDK
  • Analytics or behavioral event tracking through an analytics SDK

SecondLook does not contain advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral tracking SDKs.

5. How Google AI/Gemini Processes Your Information

SecondLook uses Google AI/Gemini services through Google's Firebase AI/Vertex AI infrastructure to perform product recognition, analysis and purchase-decision intelligence.

Depending on the feature being used, information sent for AI processing may include product images, product text, prices, budget limits, priorities and dealbreakers.

SecondLook's Gemini requests are designed not to include your Firebase user ID, name or email address as part of the product analysis prompt.

Google may process information transmitted to its AI services in accordance with Google's applicable service terms and privacy practices.

6. Firebase and Cloud Firestore

SecondLook uses Firebase services for limited authentication, usage, entitlement and security functions.

Cloud Firestore is not used as a cloud backup of your saved product reports, product photographs or personal decision profile.

The cloud information associated with SecondLook is limited to operational metadata required for authentication, entitlement and usage enforcement.

7. Data Security

Data transmitted between SecondLook and supported cloud services is protected using encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

Release versions of SecondLook also use Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to help protect backend resources from unauthorized application clients.

Local application data is protected by Android's application sandbox and private application storage mechanisms.

8. Data Retention

Product images, saved decision reports and profile information stored locally remain on your device until you remove them, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application, subject to Android's normal device behavior.

Limited Firebase authentication, usage and entitlement records are retained only as required for the application's account, entitlement and usage-management functions.

9. Account and Cloud Data Deletion

SecondLook provides a DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA function in the application's Settings section.

When successfully completed, the deletion process removes the Firebase Authentication account and associated SecondLook cloud usage records, including the applicable Firestore user and installation records.

The application also clears the applicable local entitlement and usage state associated with the deleted account.

Important: Deleting a SecondLook account does not delete your Google Account and does not cancel or delete Google Play subscriptions. Google Play manages those records independently.

Local product photographs, saved reports and other application data can be removed through the application's available data controls, Android application-data controls, or by uninstalling the application.

10. Third-Party Services

SecondLook relies on the following Google services to provide its functionality:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase AI / Google AI / Gemini
  • Google Search Grounding
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Integrity

These services are operated by Google and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

SecondLook AI is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a child-directed service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service in a manner that should not have occurred, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SecondLook's functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will always be made available at this URL.
